⚡ Flexible Cadence Engine

Supports Monthly (Electricity, WiFi), Every 2 Months (Piped gas/water), Quarterly (Society maintenance), and Annual (Property tax, Term insurance).

🔔 Unobtrusive Due Alerts

Configurable lead times (e.g. 3 days before for electricity, 15 days before for property tax). Appears as a top banner on Home screen only when approaching.

💳 1-Tap "Pay & Log" Sync

Tapping "Pay & Log" records the expense directly into DropSpend's Expenses database with category and tags, and advances the bill's cycle to the next due period automatically.

🔒 100% Free & Offline

Stored locally in localStorage (key qe_utility_reminders_v1) and synced optionally to your personal Google Sheet. No paid cloud servers needed.
