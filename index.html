        Your expenses save instantly on this device. Connect a personal Google Sheet (100% free forever) so your data is backed up and syncs across your phone and laptop.

        Leave blank for local-only mode, or paste your deployed Apps Script URL for automatic 2-way sync.
        How to set up your free Google Sheet (90 seconds)
        1. Open sheets.new to create a blank Google Sheet in your Google Drive.
        2. In the top menu, click Extensions → Apps Script.
        3. Click the button below to copy the sync script, then paste it into the Apps Script editor (replacing existing code) and press Save.
        4. Click Deploy → New deployment (top right), choose type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, and click Deploy.
        5. Copy the Web app URL and paste it into the field above!
