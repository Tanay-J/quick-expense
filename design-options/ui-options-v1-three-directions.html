DropSpend redesign: UI/UX options

3 design directions × 8 screens · Goals: easy to use, nice to look at

Rules shared by all 3 options

  1. One app, not three. The Classic / Zen / Spec layout switcher goes away. Pick a direction and commit; fewer settings means less to maintain.
  2. Three places only: Home, History, Stats. Settings sits behind your avatar. Calendar, Patterns, Explore, Compare and Fun facts move into Stats as cards.
  3. One big number per screen, written in plain words: "₹993/day left" instead of KPI grids and pace jargon.
  4. Add is always one tap away and in thumb reach. Saving takes at most 2 actions: type, then Enter.
  5. Show what was understood before saving: amount, category, payment and date as chips you can tap to change. No forms.
  6. Less chrome: no borders where spacing works, at most 1 accent colour, and category colour only on small icons.
